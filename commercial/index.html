Restaurant and Food Service Water Heaters

Restaurants and food-service businesses have hot water demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Dish machines, three-compartment sinks, prep sinks, mop sinks, and handwashing stations all draw at once during a busy dinner service, and an undersized heater runs cold right when the dish room backs up. Palm Harbor Water Heater specializes in food-service hot water, including high-input gas tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters for high-temperature dish machines, proper sizing based on peak-hour demand, and scheduled service that works around your hours.

When the main heater falls behind, handwashing sinks run lukewarm, the three-compartment sink falls below temperature, and the dish machine struggles to sanitize during the rush. We verify temperatures at the tank, the booster, and the fixtures and document every visit in written reports your inspector and landlord can review.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned with owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits the hours you're open. If a job can't be done during service, we do it after close, so the kitchen opens the next day with hot water ready.

High-Demand Facilities

Hotel, Multifamily, and Large-Facility Water Heaters

Scale changes everything. One failed tank in a hotel can leave an entire floor with lukewarm showers, and a multifamily building with a single central heater has no margin for error. Palm Harbor Water Heater provides the experience and planning these systems require, from recirculation pumps and balancing valves to multi-unit installations.

Downtime in a large building is measured in lost revenue and tenant complaints, so we plan for prevention first. Code & Inspections

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. Our technicians inspect flue draft and vent connections, verify adequate combustion air, test relief valves and their discharge lines, confirm expansion tanks are charged to the right pressure, and adjust mixing valves so water stored at higher temperatures leaves the tap at a safe temperature. We pull permits and schedule inspections where required. Findings go into a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test results, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms.

After-Hours Service

After-Hours Commercial Water Heater Response

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and spark tenant disputes. Palm Harbor Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties across Palm Harbor and surrounding areas, and we schedule non-urgent work after hours or before opening to limit downtime.

Our technicians are equipped to handle the full range of commercial water heater emergencies, including gas valve failures, burned-out heating elements, flame sensor and ignition faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks and fittings, recirculation pumps that stopped, and tankless error-code lockouts. We prioritize diagnosing the fault and restoring hot water as quickly as possible, isolating a failed unit so the rest of the system keeps running, then put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again.
